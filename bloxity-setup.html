Description
Every step gives you Speed, and Speed is what breaks glass. Run down a long corridor of giant panes: push one and its health drains at your Speed while cracks spread across it, until it bursts into hundreds of flying shards. Behind each stage a Shard Pad pays out and sends you home, and the further the stage, the more Shards. Spend them on 18 pairs of sneakers that make every step worth more, stand on treadmills to train while you watch the numbers climb (up to x35), and rebirth to multiply it all for good. Clear the last stage of a world to open the next: Glasshouse Gardens, Skyline Sprint, Frostpane Peaks and Ember Foundry, each with tougher glass and far bigger rewards. Press PLAY for Glass Rush: everyone who presses it together races through a field of shared panes, where the holes you smash are there for everyone and the giant pane at the end takes all of you. Races get longer, tougher and better paid as your Level climbs. Tap AUTO RUN and the game plays the whole loop for you. Spin the wheel, open the FREE chest, finish quests and daily tasks, claim FREE gifts and the daily reward, and top the leaderboards.

Hosting steps

  1. GitHub: create the empty repo LEGiON-Platforms/speed-to-break-glass (no README) and add the repo secret LEGION_DEPLOY_TOKEN.
  2. hosting.bloxity.io: the app speed-to-break-glass must exist (its id is GAME_ID in .github/workflows/deploy.yml).
  3. Push the code to main (as asivixlp44-cmyk): the workflow builds the server image and the client and deploys both.
  4. Check https://speed-to-break-glass.host.bloxity.io/health (it should say "db": true: Legion provides MONGODB_URI) and play at https://speed-to-break-glass.play.bloxity.io.
  5. Create the game in Manage Games with the values above (slug exactly speed-to-break-glass).
